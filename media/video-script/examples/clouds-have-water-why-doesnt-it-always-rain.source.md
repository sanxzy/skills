## Scope and rejected shortcuts

The example avoids global percentages and exact droplet dimensions: the mechanism does not need them. It does not say that air is a sponge, that a cloud “becomes too heavy,” or that every drop returns to the same puddle. It does not cover every cloud-formation pathway, precipitation type, or groundwater timescale. The brief is a general explanation of rain and subsequent water movement, not a complete meteorology course.

No external-data calculation is used. Timing and word-rate estimates are editorial calculations, not scientific findings.
