# TODO — SRT handoff to audio-script

Agreed outcome: `video-script` produces a standards-compliant `.srt` file that can be imported into a video editor's subtitle track, plus a paired `<stem>.source.md` evidence/production file. **One spoken sentence at most across one or two subtitle text lines per cue**, with sequential cue numbers and `HH:MM:SS,mmm --> HH:MM:SS,mmm` timestamps. Keep `♪ ♪` cues **in the SRT** as intentional video-track music/SFX placement markers (they may be visible when the subtitle track is displayed). `audio-script` consumes the SRT and omits `♪ ♪` from the Eleven v3 voice text, retaining its timecode in the voice map. SRT timestamps are editorial estimates, not recorded TTS timing.
